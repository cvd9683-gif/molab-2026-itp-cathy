## Progress

- Writing the final **Music Timeline** proposal and a week-by-week plan. The app is for logging the songs that defined each month of your life: search for a song (free iTunes Search API, or type one in by hand), pick the month, add a short note, and scroll a timeline with album art (plus a year view). Tap a song to see your note and hear a 30-second preview.
- Multi-user: anonymous Firebase sign-in and a display name, share your timeline to Cloud Firestore, a Friends list, likes and comments, and a **Same era** screen that shows what everyone was listening to in one month. The must-have core stays on the phone as JSON, so it works with no account.
